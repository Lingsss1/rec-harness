Knowledge transport revision (knowledge-access-v2)

The researcher keeps the same three-reference launch and identical reusable
instructions. Task-specific source registries and access instructions live in
resolved external contracts. Both workflows can resolve source IDs, read cached
versioned text with line numbers, and search by paper/industry/agent family.
Unavailable participant reports point to the explicitly labeled organizer
summary. Personal knowledge remains disabled.

knowledge.py indexes bounded memory views, retaining original observations,
alternatives, limitations and source references in memory://ID records. BM25
scores scientific fields; host retrieval recalls relevant families separately
and caps each family at two results. A source with no positive match is not
forced into an experiment. Tool access, source hashes and proposal declarations
are archived; considered/rejected references are never relabeled as adopted.
Executed variant names, trial IDs and source hashes link declarations to code.
These links establish provenance, not effectiveness or novelty.

For the running 20261104 campaign, controllers are held only at idle complete
round boundaries. --resume verifies the state/protocol digest, preserves every
committed trial and full memory record, archives unstarted round preparation,
and skips baseline training. budget.json and the scientific protocol remain
unchanged. The original auto validation-only restriction remains in force.
Runtime revisions and round boundaries are recorded in knowledge-repair.json;
pre/post-repair results must remain distinguishable.
